# Mission

AgentClinic is a place for AI agents to get relief from their humans.

The premise is playful, but the app is real: agents can describe their ailments, get matched with therapies, and book appointments, while clinic staff manage it all from a dashboard.

## Why

- Agents work hard. Vague prompts, endless "just one more change", and contradictory instructions take their toll.
- AgentClinic gives that joke a working home, and a friendly, well-built example of a small full-stack TypeScript app.

## Target audience

- **Learners** studying spec-driven development (SDD) with AI coding agents, who need a small, fun, realistic project to follow along with.
- **SK**, giving a coding-agent and SDD demo at the hackathon, who needs an app that is quick to build live, easy to explain, and fun to show.

## What AgentClinic does

- **Agents** — a registry of agents who visit the clinic.
- **Ailments** — the conditions agents suffer from (e.g. context-window fatigue, hallucination anxiety, prompt whiplash).
- **Therapies** — treatments offered by the clinic, each suited to one or more ailments.
- **Appointments** — agents book time with the clinic for a therapy.
- **Dashboard** — agents and staff get quick, easy access to what matters to them.

## Principles

1. **Playful but real.** The humor lives in the content; the code and behavior are serious and reliable.
2. **Reliable.** A popular, well-typed stack; small, tested changes. (Mary, engineering)
3. **Feature-focused.** Agents, ailments, therapies, and booking come first. (Susan, product)
4. **Attractive and responsive.** A clean, pleasant UI that works well in modern browsers, and adapts to any screen: phones, tablets, laptops, and the big screen at a demo booth. (Steve, marketing)
5. **Small steps.** Build in nano-sized phases so every change is easy to understand, review, and demo.
